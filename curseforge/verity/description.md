# Verity

Not every zombie is grumpy.

**Verity** is a zombie painted sunshine yellow from head to toe, with a big smiley face. Always happy to see you (and still a zombie, so mind your step).

## Features

- **New mob: Verity.** Bright yellow with black oval eyes and a wide grin, on the zombie model. Baby Verity too.
- **1 in 100 zombies** spawns as Verity instead.
- **Doesn't burn in sunlight**: it's made of sunshine.
- **Zombie stats**: fights like a zombie.
- **Verity Spawn Egg** in the Spawn Eggs creative tab.
- **Drops** rotten flesh and yellow dye.

Works on its own or together with **Mummy** and **Zombie Kingdom**.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader
- Fabric API

Server and client both need the mod installed.
