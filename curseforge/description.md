# Mummy

Something stirs beneath the dunes...

**Mummy** adds a new desert undead to Minecraft: a friendly-faced little monster wrapped in ancient linen, with eyes that glow in the dark. Plus zombie royalty and Verity, the smiley zombie!

## Features

- **New mob: the Mummy.** A cute green face peeking out of a bandage hood, on the zombie model. Baby mummies included.
- **Flowing bandages.** Loose strips hang from its arms and body and wave like banner cloth. They trail behind when it walks, swing when it turns and flutter up when it falls.
- **Glowing eyes**, so you'll spot them across the dunes at night.
- **Zombie stats**: 20 health, 3 attack, 2 armor. It fights like a zombie and calls reinforcements on Hard.
- **Doesn't burn in daylight.** It's a desert creature, so the sun doesn't hurt it.
- **Spawns in deserts** on the surface, alongside husks.
- **Mummy Spawn Egg** in the Spawn Eggs creative tab.
- **Drops**: rotten flesh and string (unravelled bandages), plus the zombie's rare drops.
- Counts as **undead**, so Smite works on it.

## Zombie royalty

Every now and then, a zombie rises wearing a crown.

- **1 in 100 zombies** spawns as a **Zombie King**: a cheerful, smiling monarch in a jewelled gold crown and a flowing red royal cape that billows as he walks.
- **Another 1 in 100** spawns as a **Zombie Princess**: an adorable little royal with a tiny crown and a pink dress that swishes as she walks.
- **Royal couple:** when the king and princess meet, they stay together for good, following each other like a dog and its owner. Every time they come together they spew fireworks and hearts! (Stand back: the fireworks hurt players.)
- They fight like zombies, call ordinary zombies to their aid, and **don't burn in sunlight**.
- They drop gold nuggets.
- **Spawn eggs** for both in the Spawn Eggs creative tab.

## Zombie kingdoms

Explore the plains, forests and savannas and you may find an **abandoned zombie kingdom**: a ruined village behind cobblestone castle walls, red banners flying, and a big cobblestone castle in the middle. In the throne room sit the **Zombie King** and **Zombie Princess**, guarded by a whole army:

- **Zombie Knights** in iron armour with swords and red shields, which they raise to block your blows. Some patrol on **zombie horses** in red armour.
- A **stable** full of armoured zombie horses, ready to be tamed.
- **Zombie Archers** in green hoods, shooting from the walls and towers.
- Hurt a royal and every guard nearby comes for you. Win, and the treasure chest behind the thrones is yours.

## Verity

- A sunshine-yellow zombie with a big smiley face. Always happy to see you.
- **1 in 100 zombies** spawns as Verity, and there's a **Verity Spawn Egg**.
- Doesn't burn in sunlight, and drops yellow dye.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader
- Fabric API

Server and client both need the mod installed.
